# Build roadmap

Steps in build order, with the decisions from `02_component_choices.md` folded in. Tick items as they land.

## Phase 1: Data and events

- [x] **1a. Download script.** `scripts/download_data.py`: events, phases, match JSON for all 20 matches pulled (90 MB). Tracking (1.8 GB, Git LFS via media.githubusercontent.com) also downloaded.
- [x] **1c. Schema check.** `scripts/inspect_data.py`; findings in `04_data_notes.md` (20 matches, 1,223 forward receptions, EPV, pressure labels, `high_pass`, `last_defensive_line_*`, `separation_*` all present).
- [x] **1b. Loader.** `src/holdup/loader.py` (own loader, kloppy not used; `scripts/check_loader.py` confirms attack direction matches SkillCorner for all 20 matches). Pandas events and phases keyed on `(match_id, event_id)`; stream tracking jsonl; normalise tracking so the attacking team plays +x per period (goalkeeper mean x); use pitch size from match.json.
- [x] **2. Back-line algorithm.** `src/holdup/backline.py`, running inside the detector. Compared with SkillCorner's own last-defender position in `notebooks/06_pre_receipt.ipynb`: the deepest outfield defender matches it exactly (median difference 0.00 m, correlation 1.00); our median-x back line sits about 3 m shallower on average (correlation 0.98).
- [~] **3. Hold-up detector.** `src/holdup/detector.py`, explained gate by gate (charts and tracking frames) in `notebooks/01_event_detection.ipynb`, with every threshold editable in one `PARAMS` cell. Candidates are passes to a CF/LF/RF, received or won by a defender first, restarts included. Gates: linked, ball from behind (45 degrees), on the line (band), not beyond the line. Defender closeness at first contact is no longer a rule (switch on with `require_engaged`; attacking half also optional via `min_x`), so separation from defenders can be assessed afterwards. 599 hold-ups (435 received, 164 defender first) at band 10 m; band is the main lever for volume (261 at 2.5 m).
- [x] **4. Sensitivity report.** Band x engaged grid and ball-angle, min_x, gap tables are in notebook 01 (261 to 694 by band, 435 to 928 by angle). Frame review done in `notebooks/13_frame_qa.ipynb`: no natural break at the 10 m band edge, so it stays a judgement call (see the data notes); contact sheets and a verdict template are in `reports/qa/`.
- [x] **5. Outcome labeller.** `src/holdup/outcome.py`, explained in `notebooks/02_outcomes.ipynb`. Kept, foul won, lost (defender first, possession loss, unsuccessful pass, opponent possession in window, ball out to opponent) and own shot, from SkillCorner events with a 3 s window; retention of received balls 68.5%; window sensitivity 2-5 s done (rank correlation 0.93-0.97).

## Phase 2: Features

- [x] **6. Pass difficulty.** `src/holdup/features.py`, explained in `notebooks/03_pass_difficulty.ipynb`. Distance, flight time, ball speed, peak and contact height, aerial flag (SkillCorner `high_pass` or peak above 2 m), pass angle, origin x/y, striker width. Contact and peak height are the strongest single features (AUC 0.76); peak height correlates 0.72 with SkillCorner's reception difficulty label.
- [x] **7. Pressure models.** `src/holdup/pressure.py`, compared in `notebooks/04_pressure.ipynb`: pressing intensity against the pressure oval, plus distance baselines, at release and first contact. With separation no longer gated, pressure carries real signal: the oval beats pressing intensity on both tests (label correlation 0.72 vs 0.55, cross-validated gain 0.0075 vs 0.0053), but plain distance (defenders within 5 m, nearest defender) does better than both (0.0150, 0.0101).
- [x] **8. Support options.** `src/holdup/support.py`, explained in `notebooks/05_support.ipynb`. For every team-mate within 30 m at first contact: geometric lane open, free (pressing-intensity matrix), and a physics-based expected pass completion (no training) with 60 / 70 / 80% counts. They agree with SkillCorner's passing-option-ease label (-0.46 to -0.35) and add a small consistent gain on top of pass and pressure features (best: options at 60%+ completion, -0.0064 log loss); using every support measure at once overfits.
- [x] **9. Pre-receipt actions and context.** `src/holdup/prereceipt.py`, explained in `notebooks/06_pre_receipt.ipynb`. Separation gain (nearest defender and the release-time marker), goal-side gain, check-short, lateral movement, path, speed and acceleration over the last 0.5 s, line gain and line height. Only 26% of hold-ups gain separation, and separation gain does not separate kept from lost on top of the other features; speed, acceleration, check-short and line height do (small gains).
- [x] **10. Validate features** against SkillCorner's five labels, done as section 1 of `notebooks/07_expected_retention.ipynb`: pass features align with reception difficulty (peak height 0.72), pressure with overall pressure and time to impact (oval 0.72 / 0.79, nearest defender -0.78 / -0.87), support with passing-option ease and open lanes with overall pressure. Final set: pass (aerial, peak height, flight time, distance, origin x), pressure (defenders within 5 m, oval sum), support (options at 60%+, open lanes), own state (speed, metres past the line, line height).
- [x] **11. Train xR.** `src/holdup/xr.py`, `notebooks/07_expected_retention.ipynb`. Regularised logistic regression and small boosted trees, cross-validated holding out whole matches; the average of the two is best (log loss 0.572 vs 0.698 for the base rate, AUC 0.77) and well calibrated (slope 1.01, intercept 0.01). Trained on the 583 labelled hold-ups (no need for the wider receptions pool). Out-of-fold xR saved to `data/processed/holdup_xr.csv`.
- [x] **12. RoE, set-up added, duel added.** `notebooks/08_retention_over_expected.ipynb`. xR scored at pass release and at first contact (out of fold); set-up added = xR_contact - xR_release, duel added = kept - xR_contact, RoE = their sum, centred on the league mean; the striker-controlled set-up (his speed and line position swapped for league averages) is built too. The method works, but with 22 strikers at 10-65 hold-ups each the striker-level differences in RoE, set-up and duel are within noise (random split-half correlation about 0); only the service he receives (xR at release) is stable (0.64).
- [x] **13. Value added.** `notebooks/09_value_added.ipynb`, `src/holdup/value.py`. SkillCorner EPV when he gets the ball vs EPV at his action plus one (next possession, signed by team; own-possession end for shots, fouls and dead balls); expected value added from an out-of-fold model (R2 0.14); value over expected. Value over expected has a weak positive split-half reliability across strikers (0.19, 5th-95th percentile -0.04 to 0.42), better than RoE but still not a ranking. The open-EPV-grid repeat is not run: that grid is not in this open data.

## Phase 4: Player outputs

- [x] **14. Situational splits and hierarchical model.** `notebooks/10_situations_and_shrinkage.ipynb`. Model checked in splits (open play / restart, aerial / ground, defender near / free, pass distance): calibrated everywhere except rare free hold-ups (about +3 points). Striker levels fitted with a normal random-effects model with a grid posterior on the between-striker spread; the real spread of RoE, set-up and duel is tiny next to single-hold-up luck (ICC about 0.003), value over expected a little larger (0.03), service received largest (0.18). Shrunk RoE and value estimates are almost all within a couple of points of the league mean with no striker's 90% interval excluding it.
- [x] **15. Uncertainty.** `notebooks/11_uncertainty.ipynb`, `src/holdup/uncertainty.py`. Hold-ups per 90 (minutes played and minutes of team possession, from `playing_time` in the match files) with exact Poisson intervals and a gamma-Poisson shrunk rate; 2,000-resample bootstrap 90% intervals and the shrunk estimates for RoE, set-up, duel and value over expected; league percentile ranks recomputed in 2,000 posterior draws so each carries a range. Median 90% range of a striker's percentile is 82-89 points wide, so percentiles are not reportable on their own at this sample size.
- [x] **16. Player scorecard and charts.** `notebooks/12_scorecard.ipynb`. RoE vs value over expected and set-up vs duel scatters (shrunk, 90% intervals), a one-page scorecard per rated striker (per-90 rate, four measures vs league, percentile with range, situational dots, waterfall, plain-language reading), head-to-head percentile bars, tracking frames of one of his hold-ups. All 22 rated strikers saved to `reports/scorecards/` (git-ignored). Strikers under 10 hold-ups are not rated.

- [x] **17. Clip and pitch-frame QA.** `notebooks/13_frame_qa.ipynb`. The open data has no video, so QA is on 10 fps tracking: automatic checks on all 599 hold-ups (none fails) and six contact sheets of release frames (72 events) by distance from the back line, saved to `reports/qa/` with a `review.csv` template for your verdicts.
